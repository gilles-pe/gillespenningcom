Why is change so hard in healthcare?

As populations age, the need for care grows. Data and AI open up new possibilities, and people shape what makes it into everyday care.

I enjoy 25 years in MedTech, alongside great people and projects. Here I share the projects I build, the ideas I pick up from the people I meet, and a few thoughts on what we need to sustain and improve healthcare in times of technological and demographic changes.
